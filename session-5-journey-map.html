The Step and The Yes are not the same thing. The step is the action they take to find out. The yes is what they commit to. If you wrote the same answer in both, you have four stages, not five.
Your access point is not always The Step. If your archetype is The Demonstration, it may sit at Trust. Where it lands tells you something true about how people actually come to you.
THE FOUR TESTS

Run the Whole Map Through These

Not each stage. The whole path. If one does not pass, something on the map is serving you instead of them.

TestDoes the map pass? Why or why not?
Identity
Does this path require you to show up as the person your nine words describe?
Ideal Client
Does every stage serve the specific person you named, or does one serve a wider audience?
Lane
Does this path lead to the lane you chose, or does it leave a door open to something else?
Discipline
Can you run this consistently, on a slow week, without anyone holding you to it?
